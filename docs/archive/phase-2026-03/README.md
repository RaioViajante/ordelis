# Phase 2026-03 Archive

Historical design specs and implementation plans from the upstream [MCO](https://github.com/mco-org/mco) project, written by its maintainer on 2026-03-16 for three features. They were inherited with the fork and previously lived in `docs/superpowers/`.

They are kept for auditability and are **not** current contracts. The three specs carry a **Superseded** banner, added upstream on 2026-07-29 (commit `9eff964`), that points to the [invocation runtime and artifact contract (`v1`)](../../contracts/invocation-runtime-v1.md). The plans describe the work for those designs and have no banner of their own; read them as historical too.

Contents:
- `specs/2026-03-16-diff-only-review-design.md` and `plans/2026-03-16-diff-only-review.md` (upstream commit `58ef688`)
- `specs/2026-03-16-mcp-server-design.md` and `plans/2026-03-16-mcp-server.md` (upstream commit `74d4b20`)
- `specs/2026-03-16-structured-streaming-design.md` (upstream commit `5f2174a`)

The files were moved here unchanged except for two kinds of correction that the move required: relative links in the spec banners, and the plans' pointers to their specs. Git history follows the files.
